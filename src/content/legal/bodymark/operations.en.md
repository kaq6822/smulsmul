## Before recording

Tell members why you are keeping records, how long you will keep them, who can access them, and how you may share them. Obtain any required consent. Turn off the microphone if you do not need sound, and keep other members and bystanders out of the frame.

## While keeping records

Record only what you need for lessons and use your device’s screen lock. When switching devices, check that lesson notes and their linked photos and videos open on the new device before clearing the old one. Review your operating-system backup and photo library sync settings.

## Sharing and deleting records

Before sharing, check the preview for people, backgrounds, annotations, and dates that could identify a member. Faces and physical characteristics may identify someone even when names and notes are omitted. When you no longer need a record, delete it in the app and check for copies in your photo library, backups, and the services you shared it with.

If a member requests access, correction, or deletion, the instructor or business holding the records verifies the identity or authority of the requester and handles the request under applicable law.
