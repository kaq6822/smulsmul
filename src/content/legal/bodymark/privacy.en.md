This policy covers data processed on the device by the BodyMark mobile app and information received through support inquiries. BodyMark is provided by Smulsmul, represented by Beomjun Kang (“the provider”).

## 1. Information stored on the device and why

| Information | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Member names or labels, exercise background and goals, pain or precautions, messages and instructor notes | Identify members and prepare and manage their lessons |
| Lesson date and time, session notes and observations | Keep and review lesson history by member |
| Captured or imported photos, capture times and views, photo annotations, recorded video and any recorded audio | Document lessons, compare posture photos, play back media and export images |
| App settings such as language and camera preferences | Remember user choices |

Information you enter or select and records created in BodyMark are kept in an on-device database and app-specific file storage. The app has no sign-in, transfers to the provider’s servers, built-in cloud sync, ad tracking, or usage analytics. The provider does not receive or remotely access member records stored in the app.

## 2. Device permissions

- **Camera** is used to take lesson photos and videos.
- **Microphone** is used when recording video with sound. Videos can also be recorded without sound.
- **Photo library** access is used to import selected photos or save exported images. The scope depends on your operating system and the permissions you grant.
- **Motion sensors** provide a camera level indicator on the device.

You can change permissions in device settings. Denying permission may limit the related feature. Revoking access leaves previously saved records in place.

## 3. Sharing, device transfers and backups

The provider does not disclose member or lesson records to third parties or engage third parties to process those records. If you send a file through the system share sheet, your chosen app and recipients may receive a copy. Copies saved to the photo library follow its sync and backup settings.

On Android, app data is excluded from operating-system cloud backup and may be included in device-to-device transfers. On iOS, app data may be included in device-to-device transfers or iCloud Backup, depending on your settings. These features are managed by the operating system. [Apple’s iCloud Backup information](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770)

## 4. Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until you delete them or the device removes the app’s data. When you confirm deletion of a member or lesson in the app, BodyMark removes the record and associated files inside the app. Manage exported or shared files and operating-system backup copies separately in the places where they are stored.

## 5. Member information and privacy rights

Instructors and businesses handling member information determine the purpose, scope, and retention period for their records and must have a lawful basis for capturing, storing, or sharing them. Additional requirements, such as separate consent where required by law, may apply to sensitive information including health-related notes.

Members can ask the instructor or business that holds their records for access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing. To exercise rights over support correspondence held by the provider or to raise a privacy concern, use the email below.

## 6. Support inquiries and privacy contact

If you email the provider, it receives your email address, message, and any attachments you send and uses them to review and respond to the inquiry. The provider deletes this information once the inquiry has been resolved, or after the required retention period if applicable law requires it to be kept. Please avoid attaching member photos, videos, or health records.
