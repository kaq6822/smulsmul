**ColorWalk** (the "App") values your privacy and complies with applicable privacy laws. This policy explains what information the App processes and how.

## 1. Information we collect

The App can be used **without creating an account**, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

To improve the service, the following **non-identifying information** may be collected automatically:

| Category | Items | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Usage data | Product interaction such as screens visited and features used | Service improvement, usage analytics |
| Diagnostics | Crash logs, performance data | Bug fixing, stability improvement |

## 2. Information processed on your device

- Photos and records you capture or select are **stored only on your device** and are never uploaded to our servers.
- The App may request **camera and photo library permissions** to take or import photos. Permissions are used only for those features, and you can still use other features if you decline.

## 3. Third-party services

The App may use third-party services (e.g., Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics) for usage statistics and crash reporting. These services collect data in a non-identifying form. For details, see:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 4. Data retention and deletion

- Automatically collected non-identifying data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described above.
- Photos and records stored on your device are deleted when you uninstall the App.

## 5. Your rights

You can revoke camera and photo permissions at any time in your device settings, and delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.

## 6. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce the changes on this page before they take effect.
