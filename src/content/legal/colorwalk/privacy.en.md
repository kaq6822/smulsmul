This Privacy Policy applies to **ColorWalk** (the "App"), a mobile application provided as a free service by **Beomjun Kang** (the "Service Provider"). The Service Provider values your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws.

## 1. Information we collect

The App can be used **without creating an account**, and we do not ask for or collect directly identifying information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The following information may be collected automatically when you download and use the App:

| Category | Items | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Device and environment | Operating system and version, device brand and model, platform, language setting, app version, app store, approximate region such as country and area | Service delivery, usage analytics |
| Usage data | Screens visited, date and time of visits, time spent on each screen, total time spent in the App | Usage analytics, service improvement |
| Diagnostics | Crash logs, stack traces, performance data | Bug fixing, stability improvement |
| Identifiers | App Instance ID, Firebase installation ID, and similar random identifiers generated per app installation | Deduplicating statistics, reproducing errors |
| Demographics and interests | Age range, gender, and interest categories estimated by Google (see "Google signals" below) | Audience analysis, service improvement |

### About IP addresses

Your IP address is transmitted to the third-party analytics services when the App connects to the internet, but Google Analytics **does not log or store IP addresses.** They are used only momentarily to infer approximate country- or region-level location, and are then discarded.

### Location information

The App **does not collect location information via GPS, cell towers, or Wi-Fi, and does not request location permissions.** Only the approximate country- and region-level information described above is used, and solely for aggregate usage statistics.

### Nature of the identifiers

These identifiers are **pseudonymous** — they are not linked to directly identifying information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, because they distinguish individual app installations, they are not fully anonymous, and the Service Provider treats them with the same care as personal information. If you reset your advertising identifier in your device settings, the app instance identifier is reset along with it.

### Google signals (Advertising Features)

The App uses **Google signals data collection**, one of the Google Analytics Advertising Features.

- **Feature in use**: Google signals data collection. For users who are signed in to a Google account and have turned on **Ads Personalization**, statistical reports are enriched with the age range, gender, and interest categories that Google estimates from the information it holds.
- **How identifiers are used**: The Service Provider and Google use first-party identifiers such as the App Instance ID and Firebase installation ID described above, and Google may additionally use its own advertising identifiers. The Service Provider does not identify individual users through this feature and only views the results as aggregated statistics from which no individual can be recognized.
- **How to opt out**: You may opt out of this feature at any time by any of the following means:
  - Turn off Ads Personalization in Google Ad Settings — <https://myadcenter.google.com>
  - Turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads / iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking)
  - Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on (for web browsers) — <https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout/>

## 2. Information processed on your device

- Photos and daily records you capture or select are **stored only on your device**. They are never uploaded to the Service Provider's servers or shared externally.
- The App may request **camera and photo library permissions** in order to take or import photos. These permissions are used only when you use those features, and the remaining features continue to work if you decline.

## 3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services for usage statistics and crash reporting. Each has its own privacy policy:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy documentation |
|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics | Usage analytics | <https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245> |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Crash reporting | <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy> |
| Google overall | — | <https://policies.google.com/privacy> |

Only the data described in the table above is transmitted to these services, and the Service Provider uses it solely to improve the App's performance and functionality. Google prohibits its customers from sending personally identifiable information (PII) to Google Analytics, and the Service Provider does not transmit any directly identifying information.

## 4. Disclosure of information

The Service Provider does not sell or rent your information. Automatically collected information may be disclosed in the following cases:

- When required by law, or in response to a subpoena or similar lawful legal process
- When the Service Provider believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect its rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate misuse, or respond to a government request
- When sharing with trusted service providers who work on the Service Provider's behalf, do not use the disclosed information independently, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set out in this policy

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- **Crash data**: Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash stack traces and associated identifiers (including Crashlytics installation UUIDs and Firebase installation IDs) for **90 days** before beginning the process of removing them from live and backup systems.
- **Analytics data**: In Google Analytics, event-level data is deleted automatically after **2 months** and user-level data after **14 months**. Aggregated statistical reporting data, which cannot identify an individual, is not subject to that retention period and may be kept.
- **On-device data**: Photos and records stored on your device are deleted when you uninstall the App.
- If you wish to delete information you provided directly to the Service Provider by email, contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

## 6. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

- **Opt out of collection**: Uninstalling the App stops all collection of information by the App. You can use the standard uninstall process available on your device or through the app marketplace.
- **Withdraw permissions**: You can revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time in your device settings.
- **Opt out of Advertising Features**: You can opt out of Google signals data collection at any time using the methods described under "Google signals (Advertising Features)" in Section 1.
- **Access, correction, deletion, and suspension of processing**: Contact us at the address below and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

## 7. Children's privacy

- Under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from **children under the age of 14**.
- Under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Service Provider does not use the App to knowingly solicit data from, or market to, **children under the age of 13**.
- If the Service Provider discovers that a child below these ages has provided personal information, it will be deleted immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can take the necessary action.

## 8. Security
