## Account deletion and learning-data requests

There is currently no dedicated deletion or export screen. Email with the subject **“Malkko account deletion request”** or **“Malkko privacy request”** and provide only:

- Your account username.
- The requested action: account closure, learning-record deletion, access or copy, correction, or restriction.
- A brief explanation needed to understand the scope.

After proportionate ownership checks, the Operator provides the result or any lawful limitation. Do not send a password or a full identification document in advance.

**Logout, uninstalling the app, and account deactivation are different from deleting server data.** Request deletion separately to remove server-side learning records. External AI copies, backups, and legally retained records follow the Privacy Policy.
