## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where are climbing records and photos stored?

CL-D stores climbing records and attached photos locally on your device.

### I cannot see nearby gyms.

Check that location access for CL-D is allowed in your device settings. You can continue using climbing-record features without location access.

### Can I delete or reset records?

You can delete saved data through the App's data-management feature. Deleted data may not be recoverable, so keep separate copies of important records.
