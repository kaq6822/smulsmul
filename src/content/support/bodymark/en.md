## Can I move my records to a new device?

BodyMark has no built-in backup or restore menu. Android users can use the operating system’s device-to-device transfer. On iOS, app data may be included in device-to-device transfers or iCloud Backup restores. Check that lessons and their linked photos and videos open on the new device before resetting the old one.

## Why can’t I use the camera or access photos?

Check BodyMark’s camera and photo permissions in your device settings. Recording video with sound requires microphone permission.

## What should I include in a support request?

Include your device model, operating system version, app version, the affected screen, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Blur any member names, faces, or lesson notes in screenshots before sending them.
