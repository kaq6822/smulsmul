## 🧭 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. How is today's color determined?**

> A new color is recommended daily based on information such as your location, time zone, and weather.

**Q2. Where are the photos I take stored?**

> Photos are safely stored in your device's storage and the app's internal gallery.

**Q3. I'm experiencing an error.**

> Please reinstall the app. If the error persists, please contact us via the support email.

**Q4. Can I reset my data?**

> You can delete data directly through App Settings > Data Management > "All Data" option. When deleted, all data will be permanently removed immediately.
