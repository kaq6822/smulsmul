## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where are clothing details and photos stored?

Closetimo stores clothing details, wear history, laundry history, and photos locally on your device.

### I cannot add a photo.

Allow camera or photo access for Closetimo in your device settings. You can still add clothing details without a photo.

### What happens when I delete an item?

Deleting an item may also delete its connected wear and wash history and the related locally stored photo. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
