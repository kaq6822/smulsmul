## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is my learning data stored?

Learning progress, saved words, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

### Can I continue learning on another device?

The current version does not provide accounts or cloud sync. Learning data is stored only on the device you use.

### Can I reset my learning history?

You can reset learning history from Data Management in Profile. Reset data may not be recoverable.
