**ColorWalk** is a walk-journaling app that makes each day special through "today's color."

Every morning, ColorWalk recommends a color that suits you. Take photos that match the color, save them, and record the feelings of your day in color.

More than a photo album — a new way to remember your day through color. Start walking your day with today's color.
