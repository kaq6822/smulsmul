**Malkko** is a personal English learning service built around sentences you create yourself. Practise turning your own ideas into English, beyond recognising familiar answers.

## Make a sentence. Try it in a new situation.

1. Read a short question and write your own sentence.
2. Use a hint to revise it before memorising a ready-made answer.
3. Change the subject, time, or situation and try the pattern again.
4. Come back later and see what you can recall.

For example, after making “I got up early yesterday,” you might try “Did he get up early today?” This is an illustration of the learning process. Actual prompts and feedback depend on your learning history.

## Before you use Malkko

Free conversation currently uses **text**. AI assessments and feedback support practice; they do not certify a test score or guarantee a learning outcome.

Your learning text and relevant conversation context are sent to **external AI services** to generate feedback. Avoid entering real names, contact details, health information, workplace secrets, or other information unnecessary for practice. See the Privacy Policy below for details.
