Closetimo is a personal digital wardrobe that helps you catalog clothes, look back on what you wore, and stay on top of laundry.

Add photos and essential details, browse your wardrobe by category, and check each item's care status based on its wear history and wash cycle. Recent wears and the laundry basket make wardrobe care part of a simple, natural routine.

Your clothing details and photos stay on your device, and the core experience works offline.
