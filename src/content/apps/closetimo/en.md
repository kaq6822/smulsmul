Closetimo is a personal digital wardrobe that helps you catalog clothes, look back on what you wore, and stay on top of laundry. It focuses on calmly caring for the clothes you own rather than on styling them.

## Light to add, steady to maintain

Add an item with just a photo and a name, and fill in brand, care method, and wash cycle only when you need to. For bags and accessories that don't suit wear-count tracking, turn that off and manage them manually.

## From wear to wash

Log several items worn today at once. Items that reach their wash cycle are flagged as needing care, and you can mark only the ones you choose in the laundry basket as washed in one step.

## Your data stays on your device

There is no account to create, and your clothing details and photos are stored only on your device. The core features work without an internet connection. A backup is created only when you start one, and you choose where to save it.
