**BodyMark** helps Pilates, yoga, and fitness instructors keep track of lessons and changes in posture. Take photos from the same angles before or after a lesson, or at regular intervals, so you can find and compare them at the next session.

## Record what fits the lesson

Set the actual lesson date and time and save notes, photos, or both. Take or import photos and add lines and marks to document what you observed during the session.

## Work offline

BodyMark stores member information and lesson records on your device without an account. You can preview your comparison before exporting it. Member names and notes are excluded from comparison images by default.
